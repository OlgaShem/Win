FAQ

WHAT IS WINORIO-PARTNERS?

Winorio-partners is an affiliate program, allowing you to partner with us. By promoting Winorio-partners on your website and referring players, you’ll earn a share of the revenue generated by those players through their deposits

HOW DO I GET STARTED?

Simply complete the registration form at Winorio-partners.com. Once we validate your details, you’ll have access to your affiliate account.

HOW MUCH I CAN EARN?

At Winorio-partners, we value our partners, offering you up to 60% of the revenue based on your performance. All statistics are live, real-time, and fully transparent.

WHEN DO I GET PAID?

Payments are processed in the third week of each month. Note: there’s a minimum payout threshold of €100. If your earnings are below that, your balance will carry over to the next month

HOW DOES WINORIO-PARTNERS TRACK THE PLAYERS I REFER?

As long as you use our unique tracking links and banners from the media gallery within your Winorio-partners account, all your referred players will be tracked accurately.

DOES IT COST ANYTHING TO JOIN THE WINORIO-PARTNERS AFFLIATE PROGRAM?

No, joining the Winorio-partners affiliate program is completely free.

HOW DO YOU TRACK MY PERFORMANCE?

We utilize a third-party software that provides real-time, updated stats every 24 hours, so you can track your performance and earnings.

WHAT PAYMENT METHODS DO YOU SUPPORT?

We offer payouts via Bank Transfer and Crypto.
